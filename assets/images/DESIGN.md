## Brand & Style

The design system powers an advanced companion experience for smart energy-harvesting footwear. It balances two worlds: elite athletic performance and laboratory-grade power engineering. The brand personality is electric, precision-engineered, motivating, and clean. It speaks to urban runners, eco-conscious tech adopters, and gadget enthusiasts who treat their movement as self-generated clean power.

The visual direction merges high-tech minimalism with dynamic glassmorphism and luminescent cyber-athletic accents:
- **Kinetic Radiance**: Vivid volt green and electric cyan accents mimic piezoelectric discharge, lighting up telemetry screens, circular energy gauges, and real-time charging curves.
- **Deep Obsidian & Pure Alabaster**: Dark mode delivers an immersive, stealth-hardware cockpit with glowing light pipes and frosted panels, while light mode delivers an ultra-clean, clinical-grade athletic aesthetic with crisp contrast and subtle ambient depth.
- **Micro-Technical Tactility**: Elements feel engineered—segmented bar meters, live wattage displays, telemetry chips, and physical-metaphor toggles mimic high-end hardware diagnostic monitors.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the high-voltage kinetic glow of neon volt green and hyper-luminescent cyan, set against pitch obsidian backdrops in dark mode and warm crisp chalk surfaces in light mode.

- **Primary (`#CCFF00`)**: The core kinetic energy signal. Used for live power generation status, battery charge rings, primary call-to-action buttons, active navigation pathing, and peak wattage metrics.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF`)**: Secondary power channel and shoe hardware link. Used for BLE connectivity, sensor diagnostics, voltage levels, and ambient footwear sole illumination.
- **Tertiary (`#7000FF`)**: Dynamic LED customization accents, deep energy storage indicators, and AI assistant intelligence beacons.
- **Neutral (`#0D1117`)**: Precision-tuned dark tone with a sub-perceptual cool undertone to prevent dead black flatness. Complemented in light surfaces with `#F6F8FA` and `#FFFFFF` for pristine card contrast.

Functional indicators reserve `#FF3B30` strictly for battery drain thresholds, circuit disconnects, and hardware errors, while `#30D158` handles completed sync states and optimal energy harvesting efficiency.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects precision kinetic technology. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides friendly yet modern geometric authority for editorial messaging, headers, and UI controls. **Space Grotesk** is deployed across telemetry metrics, Watt-hour readouts, step counts, voltages, and technical chip labels, imparting the aesthetic of precision hardware instrumentation.

Numerical data must use tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during live kinetic energy generation or live stepping updates. Uppercase tracking is applied to micro labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) to ensure glanceability in outdoor and high-movement scenarios.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages dual-layer ambient glow and frosted glassmorphic containers to reflect hardware LED refraction.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Pure base matte background (`#0D1117` in dark mode, `#F4F6F9` in light mode).
- **Level 1 (Telemetry Glass Cards)**: Translucent surface background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` dark, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` light) paired with `16px` backdrop blur, a fine 1px rim outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` dark, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` light), and ambient shadow `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Pods & Modals)**: Lifted cards with an extra-diffused volt or cyan colored bloom (`box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -6px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.18)`), creating the illusion of energized hardware.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Controls & Bottom Bar)**: Floating dock surfaces anchored at bottom safe areas with full backdrop filtering (`24px` blur), isolating user controls cleanly from background telemetry charts.

## Shapes

The shape system operates at **Level 2 (Rounded)** with purposeful variations across scales:
- Standard UI cards, interactive sheets, and settings modules carry `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) corners to feel sleek, modern, and ergonomic.
- Telemetry indicators, segmented switches, and action buttons transition to full pill profiles (`9999px`) to emphasize aerodynamic athletic hardware.
- Color pickers and sensor diagnostic indicators use circular tokens (`100%`) referencing internal piezo nodes and battery cell contacts.

## Components

### 1. Kinetic Gauge (Battery & Power Ring)
- **Visuals**: Concentric circular SVG track with a high-contrast glowing progress line (`#CCFF00` primary, gradient-terminating into `#00F0FF`).
- **Inner Core**: Features high-visibility `metric-xl` typography displaying the battery percentage alongside real-time harvested Watt-hours (`0.87 / 1.1 Wh`).
- **Live State**: Emits a soft radial pulse whenever the shoe's internal piezo elements detect walking or running pressure.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button**: High-visibility pill button filled with `#CCFF00`, bold dark typography (`#0D1117`), and a persistent micro-glow. Interactive states include an inward tap depression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Glass Button**: Frosted translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with a 1px perimeter border and crisp white or volt iconography.
- **Destructive/Disconnect Action**: Transparent background with a fine `#FF3B30` boundary outline and red-tinted text.

### 3. Segmented Timeframe & Mode Selectors
- Pill-shaped container filled with a soft background channel (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` light, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` dark).
- Selected segment slides smoothly via spring transition, surfaced in bright volt (`#CCFF00`) with dark typography or crisp white with subtle elevation.

### 4. Hardware Diagnostic & Metric Cards
- Modular grid units delivering single-glance stats (Voltage `4.2V`, Current `120 mA`, Output Power `0.50 W`).
- Labeled with uppercase `label-sm` Space Grotesk descriptors in subdued slate, anchored below by bold `metric-md` readouts.

### 5. LED Customization Swatches & Sliders
- Quick-select color swatches (Volt Lime, Ocean Cyan, Deep Purple, Solar Orange, Crisp White) rendered as luminous circular buttons with a 3px active indicator halo.
- Brightness slider designed as an illuminated tapered track with glowing thumb pill.

### 6. Kinetic Step Chart & Energy Histogram
- Vertical rounded bar displays representing power collected per hour.
- Active maximum output bars highlight in luminous `#CCFF00` with floating technical tooltip tags; inactive intervals rest in semi-transparent muted slate.
